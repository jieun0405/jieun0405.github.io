AI-based Educational Resource Matching for Underserved Children

This proposed research explores how artificial intelligence can improve access to educational resources for underserved children.

The proposed approach uses text embeddings, cosine similarity, and clustering to investigate personalized educational resource matching.

Proposed Methodology
  • Educational resource data collection
  • Text preprocessing and vectorization
  • Similarity-based resource matching
  • Clustering and quantitative evaluation
